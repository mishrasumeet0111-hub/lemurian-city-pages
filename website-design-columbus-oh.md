## A capital that keeps growing

Columbus is Ohio's largest city and its capital, and much of the metro's growth is in the suburbs: Dublin, Westerville, Delaware, New Albany and Pickerington. Customers there search for their own city.

Ohio State brings tens of thousands of students and staff, so many businesses serve a young, phone-first audience. Fast mobile pages, simple booking and clear prices matter more than visual flourishes.

Columbus is also a favorite test market for national brands, because its population resembles the country as a whole. Customers here are used to trying new things, so a clear offer and an easy first step work well.
