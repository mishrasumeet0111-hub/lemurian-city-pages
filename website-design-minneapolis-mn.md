# Minneapolis website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Minneapolis and across the Minneapolis metro. If you are looking for website designers in Minneapolis, Minnesota, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Minneapolis metro

Businesses across the whole Minneapolis area work with us, in Minneapolis itself and in every town around it. None of it depends on how close you are to the city.

Minneapolis sits in Hennepin County, the most populated county in the Minneapolis metro. Your customers may not, which is why we also work with businesses across the surrounding counties with the most people, Ramsey County, Dakota County, Anoka County, Washington County, Scott County, Wright County, Carver County and Sherburne County in Minnesota; and St. Croix County in Wisconsin, on exactly the same terms. The Minneapolis metro has 15 counties in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates.

## Twin Cities, two names

Minneapolis and St. Paul sit in different counties, Hennepin and Ramsey, and locals treat them as two places. If you serve both, say so. If you serve the suburbs, name them, from Bloomington and Edina to Eagan and Blaine.

Long winters shape how people use local businesses. Make sure your hours, weather closures and any delivery or pickup options are easy to find and easy to update.

The Twin Cities have a strong nonprofit and corporate-giving culture, and many local businesses work with foundations and mission-driven groups. Those buyers look for plain language about impact and clear contacts.

## What a good site does for a Minneapolis business
