# Website design for Raleigh businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Raleigh and across the Raleigh metro. If you are looking for website designers in Raleigh, North Carolina, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Raleigh metro

Businesses across the whole Raleigh area work with us, in Raleigh itself and in every town around it. None of it depends on how close you are to the city.

Customers of a business in Wake County, the most populated county in the Raleigh metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: Johnston County and Franklin County. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the counties where most of the Raleigh metro lives. Between them, they make up the whole Raleigh metro.

## The Triangle, named the way customers search

Raleigh is one corner of the Research Triangle, with Durham and Chapel Hill. Federal statistics put Durham in a separate metro, but customers think of the Triangle as one area. If you serve all of it, say so.

Wake County holds most of the Raleigh metro's people, in Cary, Apex, Wake Forest and Holly Springs, and Johnston County is growing around Clayton and Smithfield.

## The cost of being hard to find in Raleigh
