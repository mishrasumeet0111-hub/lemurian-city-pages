# Website design for Phoenix businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Phoenix and across the Phoenix metro. If you are looking for website designers in Phoenix, Arizona, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Phoenix metro

All of the Phoenix metro is covered, so a shop on the outskirts gets the same attention as one in the middle of Phoenix.

Phoenix sits in Maricopa County, the most populated county in the Phoenix metro. The metro's other county is Pinal County, and we work with businesses there on exactly the same terms.

## A metro that keeps spreading

Much of the Phoenix metro's growth is at its edges: Buckeye and Surprise to the west, Queen Creek and San Tan Valley to the southeast. If you serve those areas, name them. People there search for their own town, not Phoenix.

Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so for part of the year it lines up with Pacific Time and for the rest with Mountain Time. Post your hours in plain local time and keep your booking tool on Arizona time, or customers will turn up an hour off.

Summer heat changes when people go out. If your hours move to early mornings and evenings in the hottest months, post that on your site as well as on your Google profile.

## The cost of being hard to find in Phoenix

Most of the customers you are missing are the ones who went looking for a business like yours and found somebody else instead. A proper website closes that gap.
