# Website design for Buffalo businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Buffalo and across the Buffalo metro. If you are looking for website designers in Buffalo, New York, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Buffalo metro

The whole Buffalo region is served, city and suburbs alike. A business twenty miles out gets built exactly as carefully as one downtown.

We serve businesses in Erie County, the most populated county in the Buffalo metro, and in Niagara County, the only other county in the metro. Plenty of customers live in one and work or shop in the other, so a good site speaks to both.

## Erie and Niagara, plus Canada

Many Buffalo businesses see customers from southern Ontario as well as Erie and Niagara County. If you serve Canadian visitors, say so, and make prices and shipping clear for them.

Winter weather closes businesses more often here than in most cities. A notice bar you can switch on yourself, with closures and changed hours, saves customers wasted trips.

Game days and summer waterfront crowds make for busy weekends. Restaurants and bars benefit from a site where game-day hours and specials take a minute to change.

## Why a Buffalo business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.

## A question from Buffalo businesses

### Should my site show prices in Canadian dollars?

If Canadian customers are a real share of your business, a note on currency and cross-border shipping helps. Online stores can show both currencies; service businesses usually need only a clear note.
