## Fort Worth is not Dallas

Fort Worth is a big city in its own right and the seat of Tarrant County, and its customers often skip results that only say Dallas. A Fort Worth business should say Fort Worth clearly, along with the Tarrant County cities it serves, such as Arlington, Keller, Mansfield and Benbrook.

The city keeps its Western identity through the Stockyards, the rodeo and its cattle heritage, and the tourism around them is real business. Visitor-facing sites need events, hours and booking up front.

Fort Worth is growing quickly to the north, around Alliance, and to the west, and new residents are still choosing their go-to businesses. A clear site with hours, prices and reviews helps you get picked early.
