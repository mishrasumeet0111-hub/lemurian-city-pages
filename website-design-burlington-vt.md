## Small city, careful customers

Burlington is Vermont's largest city, and it is still small. Customers know local businesses by name and reputation, so a site's job is to confirm what they heard: who you are, real photos, and how to reach you.

Customers come from South Burlington, Essex, Colchester, Winooski and St. Albans, and seasonal visitors arrive for Lake Champlain summers and ski season, so seasonal hours should be easy to update.

The University of Vermont brings thousands of students each fall, and many local businesses follow its calendar. Student offers and changing hours should be quick to post yourself.

## What a good site does for a Burlington business

Customers in Burlington search before they buy, and a business they cannot find online is a business they never call. A clear site makes you visible to them and brings the work in.
