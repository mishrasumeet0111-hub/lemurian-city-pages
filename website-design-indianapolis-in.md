Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Indianapolis and across the Indianapolis metro. If you are looking for website designers in Indianapolis, Indiana, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Indianapolis metro

The whole Indianapolis region is served, city and suburbs alike. A business twenty miles out gets built exactly as carefully as one downtown.

Customers of a business in Marion County, the most populated county in the Indianapolis metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: Hamilton County, Hendricks County, Johnson County, Madison County, Hancock County, Boone County, Morgan County, Shelby County and Brown County. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the counties where most of the Indianapolis metro lives. The Indianapolis metro has 11 counties in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates.

## Marion County and the ring around it

Indianapolis and Marion County share a consolidated government, but much of the metro's growth is in the counties around it, especially Hamilton County, home to Carmel, Fishers and Noblesville. Customers there search for their own town first.

The city hosts big sporting events and conventions, so visitor-facing businesses see sudden spikes. A site you can update yourself, with event specials and hours, lets you react to them.
