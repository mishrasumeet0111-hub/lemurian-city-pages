# Orlando website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Orlando and across the Orlando metro. If you are looking for website designers in Orlando, Florida, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Orlando metro

Every part of the Orlando metro is served the same way, whether your business sits in downtown Orlando or an hour out. Location makes no difference to the work.

Our Orlando service area starts in Orange County, the most populated county in the Orlando metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Seminole County, Osceola County and Lake County. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Orlando metro. That takes in places like Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Sanford, Winter Park and Clermont.

## Websites for customers who are new to town

Many Orlando businesses serve two audiences: people who live here and visitors who do not know the roads. A visitor on a phone needs the address, parking, hours and booking link in the first screen, not three taps down.

The metro also runs south into Osceola County and north into Seminole County. If you serve Kissimmee, St. Cloud or Sanford, say so in plain words on your site. People there search for their own town name as often as they search for Orlando.
