# A web design company for Mesa and nearby counties

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Mesa and across the Phoenix-Mesa metro. If you are looking for website designers in Mesa, Arizona, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Phoenix-Mesa metro

From Mesa itself out to the smallest towns in the metro, every business gets the same service. Being outside the city limits costs you nothing here.

We serve businesses in Maricopa County, the most populated county in the Phoenix-Mesa metro, and in Pinal County, the only other county in the metro. Plenty of customers live in one and work or shop in the other, so a good site speaks to both.

## The East Valley is its own market

Mesa has more than half a million residents, yet many people outside Arizona assume it is a Phoenix suburb. Customers in the East Valley, in Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler and Queen Creek, usually search by their own city first. A Mesa business should say Mesa clearly and name the East Valley cities it serves.

Many East Valley businesses serve families and retirees, and both groups do nearly everything on their phones. Large type, simple navigation and a tap-to-call button do more for them than any visual effect.

## What a good site does for a Mesa business

People trust what they can see. A business with no site, or a tired one, gets passed over by customers who would happily have bought from it.
