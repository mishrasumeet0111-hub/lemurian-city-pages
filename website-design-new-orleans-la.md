# New Orleans website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in New Orleans and across the New Orleans metro. If you are looking for website designers in New Orleans, Louisiana, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the New Orleans metro

All of the New Orleans metro is covered, so a shop on the outskirts gets the same attention as one in the middle of New Orleans.

Customers of a business in Orleans Parish, the second most populated parish in the New Orleans metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: Jefferson Parish, St. Charles Parish, St. Bernard Parish, St. John the Baptist Parish, Plaquemines Parish and St. James Parish. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the parishes where most of the New Orleans metro lives. Between them, they make up the whole New Orleans metro.

## Parishes, neighborhoods and visitors

Louisiana has parishes, not counties, and the New Orleans metro's most populated is Jefferson Parish, home to Metairie and Kenner. Locals also search by neighborhood: the French Quarter, Uptown, Mid-City, the Marigny.

Tourism and events drive a lot of local business, and so does hurricane season. A site you can update yourself, with notices for storm closures and special-event hours, is worth having from day one.

## The cost of being hard to find in New Orleans

Every day without a decent site is customers handed to a competitor for free. Fixing that is usually the cheapest growth a small business can buy.
