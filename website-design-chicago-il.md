# A web design company for Chicago and nearby counties

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Chicago and across the Chicago metro. If you are looking for website designers in Chicago, Illinois, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Chicago metro

From Chicago itself out to the smallest towns in the metro, every business gets the same service. Being outside the city limits costs you nothing here.

Our Chicago service area starts in Cook County, the most populated county in the Chicago metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: DuPage County, Lake County, Will County, Kane County, McHenry County, Kendall County and DeKalb County in Illinois; and Lake County and Porter County in Indiana. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. The Chicago metro has 13 counties in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates. That takes in places like Naperville, Elgin, Aurora, Joliet, Evanston and Gary.

## City neighborhoods and the suburbs

Chicago customers often search by neighborhood, such as Lincoln Park, Pilsen or Wicker Park, and suburban customers search by town, such as Naperville, Evanston or Joliet. If you have a storefront, your neighborhood belongs in your page title and first screen, not just "Chicago."

The metro also crosses into northwest Indiana, through Lake and Porter County, home to Gary, Hammond and Valparaiso. If you serve that side of the line, say so, along with any state licensing that applies there.
