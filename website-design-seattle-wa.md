# Seattle website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Seattle and across the Seattle metro. If you are looking for website designers in Seattle, Washington, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Seattle metro

The whole Seattle region is served, city and suburbs alike. A business twenty miles out gets built exactly as carefully as one downtown.

We serve businesses in King County, the most populated county in the Seattle metro, and in the most populated counties around it: Pierce County and Snohomish County. Customers rarely stop at a county line, so neither do we. Between them, they make up the whole Seattle metro.

## Eastside, South Sound and the city

Customers think in cities and neighborhoods: Bellevue and Redmond on the Eastside, Tacoma to the south, Everett to the north, Ballard and Capitol Hill in the city. Name the ones you serve.

Amazon and Microsoft make the region one of the most tech-heavy in the country, and customers here book and buy online by default. If you take appointments or orders, make that the first thing a visitor can do.

Water, hills and traffic make distance matter here, so customers care which side of the lake or the Sound you are on. Make your location and coverage obvious.

## Why a Seattle business needs a site that works

Plenty of good Seattle businesses stay small only because nobody can find them. Being easy to find, and looking worth calling once you are found, is what changes that.
