Businesses across the whole Louisville area work with us, in Louisville itself and in every town around it. None of it depends on how close you are to the city.

## A metro across the Ohio River

Louisville's metro reaches into southern Indiana, through Clark and Floyd County, home to Jeffersonville and New Albany. If you serve customers on both sides of the river, say so plainly, and list any state licenses that apply.
