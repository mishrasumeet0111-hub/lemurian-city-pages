## Websites that hold up to technical buyers

Huntsville's economy leans on aerospace, defense contracting and engineering, around Redstone Arsenal and Cummings Research Park. Plenty of local businesses sell to engineers or to the companies that employ them, and those buyers read closely. Specific capabilities, certifications and plain specs persuade them more than slogans.

The city of Madison and Athens, in Limestone County, have both grown alongside Huntsville. If you serve them, name them, because people there search for their own city first.

## Why a Huntsville business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.
