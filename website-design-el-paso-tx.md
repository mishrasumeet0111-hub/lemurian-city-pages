## Web design across the El Paso metro

Every part of the El Paso metro is served the same way, whether your business sits in downtown El Paso or an hour out. Location makes no difference to the work.

El Paso sits in El Paso County, the most populated county in the El Paso metro. The metro's other county is Hudspeth County, and we work with businesses there on exactly the same terms.

## A border city on Mountain Time

El Paso is on Mountain Time, unlike most of Texas, and it sits on the border with Ciudad Juarez. Many businesses serve customers from both sides, often in Spanish, so real Spanish pages are worth planning from the start.

Fort Bliss brings a large military community, and families moving in tend to research businesses online first. Make prices, hours and location easy to find.

Many customers cross the border regularly for work, family or shopping, and many prefer WhatsApp to a phone call. If that sounds like your customers, a WhatsApp button and clear payment details help.

## The cost of being hard to find in El Paso
