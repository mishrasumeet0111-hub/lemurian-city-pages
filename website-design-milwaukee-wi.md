# Website design for Milwaukee businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Milwaukee and across the Milwaukee metro. If you are looking for website designers in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Milwaukee metro

Every part of the Milwaukee metro is served the same way, whether your business sits in downtown Milwaukee or an hour out. Location makes no difference to the work.

Our Milwaukee service area starts in Milwaukee County, the most populated county in the Milwaukee metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Waukesha County, Washington County and Ozaukee County. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Milwaukee metro.

## The city and the WOW counties

Locals call Waukesha, Ozaukee and Washington County the WOW counties, and each has towns and shopping districts of its own, such as Brookfield, Mequon and West Bend. Wauwatosa sits just west of the city in Milwaukee County. If you serve any of them, use those names on your site.

Manufacturing is still a big employer, and so are the breweries and restaurants that bring visitors downtown and to the Third Ward. Restaurants need menus and hours they can change themselves; manufacturers need clear capability pages.

Summerfest and the lakefront festival season bring big summer crowds, so restaurants and venues need hours, events and menus they can change quickly.
