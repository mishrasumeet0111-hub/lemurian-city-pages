Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in St. Louis and across the St. Louis metro. If you are looking for website designers in St. Louis, Missouri, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the St. Louis metro

From St. Louis itself out to the smallest towns in the metro, every business gets the same service. Being outside the city limits costs you nothing here.

We serve businesses in the City of St. Louis, the third most populated jurisdiction in the St. Louis metro, and in the most populated jurisdictions around it: St. Louis County, St. Charles County, Jefferson County, Franklin County, Lincoln County and Warren County in Missouri; and Madison County, St. Clair County and Macoupin County in Illinois. Customers rarely stop at a city or county line, so neither do we. The St. Louis metro has 15 jurisdictions in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates.

## The city and the county are separate

The City of St. Louis split from St. Louis County in 1876, and the two are still separate. The county has far more people, in places like Clayton, Kirkwood and Florissant, so a business that serves both should name both.

The metro crosses the Mississippi into Illinois. If you serve the Metro East, say so plainly, and list any licenses that differ between the states.

## Why a St. Louis business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.
