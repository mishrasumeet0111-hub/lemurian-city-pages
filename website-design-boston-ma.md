# Boston website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Boston and across the Boston metro. If you are looking for website designers in Boston, Massachusetts, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Boston metro

The whole Boston region is served, city and suburbs alike. A business twenty miles out gets built exactly as carefully as one downtown.

Our Boston service area starts in Suffolk County, the third most populated county in the Boston metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Middlesex County, Essex County, Norfolk County and Plymouth County in Massachusetts; and Rockingham County and Strafford County in New Hampshire. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Boston metro.

## Boston is small. Its metro is not.

Suffolk County is Boston plus Chelsea, Revere and Winthrop. Cambridge, Somerville, Newton and Quincy are separate cities, and their residents search that way, so name the ones you serve.

The metro also crosses into southern New Hampshire. If you serve customers there, say so, along with any licenses that apply in each state.

Universities and hospitals shape the city, and many businesses serve students, researchers or medical staff who arrive each September. A site you can update before move-in season, with student offers or new-patient details, pays off.

## Being found by Boston customers

When someone in Boston needs what you sell, they look online first. Without a site you are invisible at that moment, and with a good one you are the obvious choice.
