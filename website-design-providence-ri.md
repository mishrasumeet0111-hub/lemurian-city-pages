Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Providence and across the Providence metro. If you are looking for website designers in Providence, Rhode Island, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Providence metro

From Providence itself out to the smallest towns in the metro, every business gets the same service. Being outside the city limits costs you nothing here.

Providence sits in Providence County, the most populated county in the Providence metro. Your customers may not, which is why we also work with businesses across the surrounding counties with the most people, Kent County, Washington County, Newport County and Bristol County in Rhode Island; and Bristol County in Massachusetts, on exactly the same terms. Between them, they make up the whole Providence metro.

## A small state with a cross-border metro

The Providence metro takes in all of Rhode Island plus Bristol County, Massachusetts, which includes Fall River, New Bedford and Attleboro. If you serve both states, say so, and list any licenses that apply in each.

Food is a big part of the city, helped by a culinary school and a strong dining scene. Menus, hours and reservations are the pages that matter most, and you should be able to change them yourself.
