# Nashville website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Nashville and across the Nashville metro. If you are looking for website designers in Nashville, Tennessee, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Nashville metro

Every part of the Nashville metro is served the same way, whether your business sits in downtown Nashville or an hour out. Location makes no difference to the work.

We serve businesses in Davidson County, the most populated county in the Nashville metro, and in the most populated counties around it: Rutherford County, Williamson County, Sumner County, Wilson County, Maury County, Robertson County, Dickson County, Cheatham County and Macon County. Customers rarely stop at a county line, so neither do we. The Nashville metro has 14 counties in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates. That takes in places like Franklin, Murfreesboro, Hendersonville, Gallatin, Lebanon and Columbia.

## Sites that keep up with a calendar

A lot of Nashville businesses run on dates: shows, events, classes and seasonal menus. If that is you, the most useful thing a website can do is let you add and remove dates yourself, with past events dropping off automatically so the site never looks abandoned.

Davidson County is consolidated with the city, but Franklin, Murfreesboro and Hendersonville each have their own identity. Customers there search for their own town. Name the ones you serve.

## Why a Nashville business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.
