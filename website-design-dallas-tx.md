Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Dallas and across the Dallas metro. If you are looking for website designers in Dallas, Texas, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Dallas metro

From Dallas itself out to the smallest towns in the metro, every business gets the same service. Being outside the city limits costs you nothing here.

We serve businesses in Dallas County, the most populated county in the Dallas metro, and in the most populated counties around it: Tarrant County, Collin County, Denton County, Ellis County, Johnson County, Kaufman County, Parker County, Rockwall County and Hunt County. Customers rarely stop at a county line, so neither do we. The Dallas metro has 11 counties in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates. That takes in places like Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Frisco, Denton and Irving.

## Dallas and Fort Worth are not the same search

DFW is one metro with two big cities, and people on the Fort Worth side often skip results that only say "Dallas." If you serve both, put both in your page titles and service-area section. If you serve only one, say which, so you do not get calls from across the metroplex.

Many of the fastest-growing places in the area, such as Frisco, Plano and Denton, sit north of Dallas in Collin and Denton County. If that is where your customers live, name those cities, not just the metro.

Dallas is also a big corporate headquarters city, so many local firms sell to large companies. Those buyers expect named contacts, clear credentials and proof of similar work.
