# A web design company for Charlotte and nearby counties

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Charlotte and across the Charlotte metro. If you are looking for website designers in Charlotte, North Carolina, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Charlotte metro

Every part of the Charlotte metro is served the same way, whether your business sits in downtown Charlotte or an hour out. Location makes no difference to the work.

Our Charlotte service area starts in Mecklenburg County, the most populated county in the Charlotte metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Union County, Cabarrus County, Gaston County, Iredell County, Rowan County and Lincoln County in North Carolina; and York County, Lancaster County and Chester County in South Carolina. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. The Charlotte metro has 11 counties in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates. That takes in places like Concord, Gastonia, Huntersville, Mooresville and Rock Hill.

## A metro that crosses a state line

The Charlotte metro takes in three South Carolina counties: York, Lancaster and Chester. If you serve Rock Hill or Fort Mill as well as Charlotte, your site should say you work in both states, because customers on the South Carolina side often assume a Charlotte business will not cross the line.

If your trade is licensed by the state, such as contractors, real estate or many health services, list your license for each state you work in. A license number on the page is one of the strongest trust signals a local service business can show.
