## Neighborhoods, beach towns and North County

San Diego County runs from Oceanside and Carlsbad in North County to Chula Vista at the border. Customers search by neighborhood or beach town, such as La Jolla, North Park or Encinitas, so a storefront should name its own area first.

The region has a large military presence and many cross-border customers. If you serve customers in Spanish, plan real Spanish pages rather than a translation widget.

Conventions and tourists add a second audience downtown and near the beaches. Give visitors a short path to directions and booking without burying what your regulars come for.

## Why a San Diego business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.
