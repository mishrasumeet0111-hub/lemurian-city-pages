# Fargo website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Fargo and across the Fargo metro. If you are looking for website designers in Fargo, North Dakota, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Fargo metro

All of the Fargo metro is covered, so a shop on the outskirts gets the same attention as one in the middle of Fargo.

We serve businesses in Cass County, the most populated county in the Fargo metro, and in Clay County in Minnesota, the only other county in the metro. Plenty of customers live in one and work or shop in the other, so a good site speaks to both.

## Fargo-Moorhead, two states

The Fargo metro crosses the Red River into Minnesota, with Moorhead in Clay County. If you work on both sides, spell that out, with North Dakota and Minnesota license numbers where your trade needs them.

Fargo is home to North Dakota State University, and agriculture technology and health care are big employers. Clear, fast sites with plain explanations suit customers who are short on time.

Winters here are long and hard, so customers check whether a business is open before driving. A weather-closure notice you control, next to clear hours, saves them a trip in the cold.

## Why a Fargo business needs a site that works

Plenty of good Fargo businesses stay small only because nobody can find them. Being easy to find, and looking worth calling once you are found, is what changes that.
