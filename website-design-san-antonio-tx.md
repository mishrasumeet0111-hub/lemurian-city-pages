# San Antonio website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in San Antonio and across the San Antonio metro. If you are looking for website designers in San Antonio, Texas, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the San Antonio metro

Anywhere in the San Antonio area is fair game, whether you trade in San Antonio proper or out in the surrounding towns. The distance between us is never the point.

Customers of a business in Bexar County, the most populated county in the San Antonio metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: Comal County, Guadalupe County, Medina County, Wilson County, Atascosa County, Kendall County and Bandera County. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the counties where most of the San Antonio metro lives. Between them, they make up the whole San Antonio metro.

## Bilingual, military and spread out

San Antonio has a Hispanic majority, and many local businesses serve customers in both English and Spanish. Real Spanish pages, not a translation widget, are worth planning from the start.

Joint Base San Antonio makes the military a big part of the city, and service families who are relocating often look businesses up before they arrive. New Braunfels, Schertz and Boerne have grown fast too; name them if you serve them.

The River Walk and the Alamo bring millions of visitors downtown, and many pick a restaurant or tour on their phone on the spot. Fast mobile pages with hours, menus and booking at the top catch them.

## The cost of being hard to find in San Antonio
