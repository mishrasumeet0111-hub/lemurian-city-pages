# Website design for Washington businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Washington and across the Washington metro. If you are looking for website designers in Washington, District of Columbia, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Washington metro

Anywhere in the Washington area is fair game, whether you trade in Washington proper or out in the surrounding towns. The distance between us is never the point.

Our Washington service area starts in the District of Columbia, the fourth most populated jurisdiction in the Washington metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated jurisdictions: Fairfax County, Prince William County, Loudoun County, Arlington County and Stafford County in Virginia; and Montgomery County, Prince George's County, Frederick County and Charles County in Maryland. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. The Washington metro has 23 jurisdictions in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates.

## Serving the District, Maryland and Virginia

The Washington metro crosses the District and three states, and customers search by where they live: Arlington, Alexandria, Bethesda, Silver Spring, Fairfax. Most businesses here serve more than one jurisdiction, so the site should say which, and list any licenses that differ between them.

Much of the region's work involves the federal government, associations and nonprofits. Those buyers scan for credentials, past work and accessibility, so building to accessibility standards from the start is worth it.

### Can you build an accessible site for working with federal clients?

Yes. We build to WCAG 2.1 AA, which covers the WCAG 2.0 AA standard Section 508 refers to, and test with a keyboard and a screen reader before launch.
