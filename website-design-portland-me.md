Anywhere in the Portland area is fair game, whether you trade in Portland, ME proper or out in the surrounding towns. The distance between us is never the point.

## Year-round customers and summer visitors

Portland is Maine's largest city, and many businesses here run on two calendars: year-round customers and summer visitors. The site needs to work for both, with seasonal hours, reservations and offers you can change yourself.

Customers come from South Portland and Westbrook as well as Saco, Biddeford and Brunswick. Name the towns you actually serve.

The city's food scene draws diners from across New England, and many decide where to eat on their phones while walking. Menus, hours and a booking link sitting right there on the first screen turn those searches into tables.

## Why a Portland, ME business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.
