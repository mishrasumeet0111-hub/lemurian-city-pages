## Web design across the Bridgeport metro

Every part of the Bridgeport metro is served the same way, whether your business sits in downtown Bridgeport or an hour out. Location makes no difference to the work.

Bridgeport sits in the Greater Bridgeport Planning Region, the second most populated planning region in the Bridgeport metro. The metro's other planning region is the Western Connecticut Planning Region, and we work with businesses there on exactly the same terms.

## Connecticut's planning regions

Connecticut replaced its counties with planning regions for federal statistics, so the Bridgeport metro is now officially two planning regions. Customers do not search that way. They search for Bridgeport, Stamford, Norwalk, Danbury or Fairfield, and your site should use those names.

Many people in the area work in or commute toward New York, and they compare local businesses with what they see there. A sharp, fast site helps a local firm hold its own.
