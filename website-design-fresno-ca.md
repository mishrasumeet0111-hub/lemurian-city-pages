## Agriculture next door

Fresno sits in the middle of the San Joaquin Valley, one of the most productive farming regions in the country. Many local businesses sell to growers, packers and food companies, and those buyers want specifics: what you supply, where you deliver and how to order.

Clovis sits right next to Fresno and has its own identity. Many customers in the area prefer Spanish, so a bilingual site with real translated pages can reach people a single-language site misses.

Fresno is also a gateway to Yosemite, Kings Canyon and Sequoia, so some businesses see summer visitors passing through. Hours, directions and phone-friendly booking help catch them.
