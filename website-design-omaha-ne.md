# Website design for Omaha businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Omaha and across the Omaha metro. If you are looking for website designers in Omaha, Nebraska, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Omaha metro

We work with businesses everywhere in the Omaha metro, from the center of Omaha to the towns at the far edge of it. Where you are based does not change what we can build for you.

Our Omaha service area starts in Douglas County, the most populated county in the Omaha metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Sarpy County, Cass County, Saunders County and Washington County in Nebraska; and Pottawattamie County, Mills County and Harrison County in Iowa. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Omaha metro.

## Nebraska and Iowa, one metro

The Omaha metro crosses the Missouri River into Iowa, where Council Bluffs faces downtown. If you take customers from the Iowa side, put that on the site in plain words; people there may assume an Omaha business will not make the trip.

Sarpy County, home to Bellevue, Papillion and La Vista, is the second most populated county in the metro. Offutt Air Force Base near Bellevue brings service families who move in and look businesses up before they arrive.

Omaha is home to large insurance, finance and rail companies, so many local firms sell to corporate buyers. They expect clear credentials, named contacts and a site that looks maintained.

## Being found by Omaha customers

When someone in Omaha needs what you sell, they look online first. Without a site you are invisible at that moment, and with a good one you are the obvious choice.
