# Website design for Houston businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Houston and across the Houston metro. If you are looking for website designers in Houston, Texas, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Houston metro

All of the Houston metro is covered, so a shop on the outskirts gets the same attention as one in the middle of Houston.

Our Houston service area starts in Harris County, the most populated county in the Houston metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Fort Bend County, Montgomery County, Brazoria County, Galveston County, Liberty County, Waller County, Chambers County, Austin County and San Jacinto County. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Houston metro. That takes in places like Pasadena, The Woodlands, Sugar Land, Katy, Pearland and Galveston.

## Spread out, so be specific

The Houston metro is spread out across ten counties, and few customers will drive across it for a local service. A plumber in Katy and a dentist in Pearland are not competing for the same people. Name the areas you actually serve, and put your address or service area where a phone visitor sees it first.

Many Houston businesses serve customers in English and Spanish. If you do, plan real Spanish pages from the start rather than a translation widget, so search engines can index them and customers can trust them.

Gulf storms and flooding can close a business without warning. A site you can update yourself, with a notice bar for closures, is worth building in from day one.
