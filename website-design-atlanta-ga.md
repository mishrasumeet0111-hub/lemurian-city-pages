# A web design company for Atlanta and nearby counties

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Atlanta and across the Atlanta metro. If you are looking for website designers in Atlanta, Georgia, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Atlanta metro

Businesses across the whole Atlanta area work with us, in Atlanta itself and in every town around it. None of it depends on how close you are to the city.

Customers of a business in Fulton County, the most populated county in the Atlanta metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: Gwinnett County, Cobb County, DeKalb County, Clayton County, Cherokee County, Forsyth County, Henry County, Paulding County and Coweta County. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the counties where most of the Atlanta metro lives. The Atlanta metro has 29 counties in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates. That takes in places like Sandy Springs, Roswell, Marietta, Decatur, Lawrenceville and Alpharetta.

## A 29-county metro needs a clear service area

The Atlanta metro covers 29 counties, so "serving Atlanta" can mean almost anything. Say exactly where you work: inside the Perimeter, a few suburbs such as Marietta or Alpharetta, or the whole region. A clear service area brings fewer wasted calls and better leads.

With that many counties, a long list of town names in the footer looks like spam to visitors and to Google. Put the service area in one honest section, and give a place its own page only when you have real work or a real location there.
