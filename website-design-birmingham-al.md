# Birmingham website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Birmingham and across the Birmingham metro. If you are looking for website designers in Birmingham, Alabama, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Birmingham metro

Every part of the Birmingham metro is served the same way, whether your business sits in downtown Birmingham or an hour out. Location makes no difference to the work.

Birmingham sits in Jefferson County, the most populated county in the Birmingham metro. Your customers may not, which is why we also work with businesses across the surrounding counties with the most people, Shelby County, St. Clair County, Walker County, Blount County, Chilton County and Bibb County, on exactly the same terms. Between them, they make up the whole Birmingham metro.

## Medical and professional services, explained plainly

Birmingham is a major medical center, anchored by UAB, and much of the region's work is in health care, banking and professional services. Sites in those fields carry a lot of information, and the job is to make it easy to scan: who you help, what it costs, how to book.

Shelby County, south along I-65 through Hoover and Alabaster, is the metro's second most populated county. Customers there often search for their own city, so name it if you serve it.

If you serve the Over the Mountain suburbs, such as Mountain Brook, Vestavia Hills and Homewood, those names matter more to customers there than Birmingham itself.

## What a good site does for a Birmingham business

Customers in Birmingham search before they buy, and a business they cannot find online is a business they never call. A clear site makes you visible to them and brings the work in.
