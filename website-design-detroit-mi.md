# A web design company for Detroit and nearby counties

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Detroit and across the Detroit metro. If you are looking for website designers in Detroit, Michigan, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Detroit metro

We work with businesses everywhere in the Detroit metro, from the center of Detroit to the towns at the far edge of it. Where you are based does not change what we can build for you.

Customers of a business in Wayne County, the most populated county in the Detroit metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: Oakland County, Macomb County, Livingston County, St. Clair County and Lapeer County. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the counties where most of the Detroit metro lives. Between them, they make up the whole Detroit metro.

## City, suburbs and the counties between

Wayne County holds Detroit, but Oakland and Macomb County together hold more of the metro's people. Customers in Troy, Southfield, Warren or Sterling Heights search for their own city, so name the ones you serve.

New small businesses keep opening downtown and in neighborhoods such as Corktown and Midtown. A site for one of them should lead with the neighborhood, because that is how locals describe where things are.

Michigan winters push many service businesses to change what they offer by season, from roofing and landscaping to snow removal. The site should show what you do now, not what you did in July.

## The cost of being hard to find in Detroit

Most of the customers you are missing are the ones who went looking for a business like yours and found somebody else instead. A proper website closes that gap.
