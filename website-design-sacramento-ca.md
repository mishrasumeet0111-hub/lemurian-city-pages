# Website design for Sacramento businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Sacramento and across the Sacramento metro. If you are looking for website designers in Sacramento, California, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Sacramento metro

From Sacramento itself out to the smallest towns in the metro, every business gets the same service. Being outside the city limits costs you nothing here.

We serve businesses in Sacramento County, the most populated county in the Sacramento metro, and in the most populated counties around it: Placer County, Yolo County and El Dorado County. Customers rarely stop at a county line, so neither do we. Between them, they make up the whole Sacramento metro.

## State capital, growing suburbs

As California's capital, Sacramento has many businesses that serve government agencies, associations and the people who work with them. Those visitors want credentials and contact details quickly, so build the site around what they came to do.

Much of the metro's growth is outside the city: Roseville in Placer County, Folsom and Elk Grove in Sacramento County, and Davis in Yolo County. Name the places you serve rather than relying on Sacramento alone.

Plenty of residents have moved from the Bay Area in recent years, and they tend to look a business up online before calling. A polished, quick site makes that first impression count.

## Why a Sacramento business needs a site that works

Plenty of good Sacramento businesses stay small only because nobody can find them. Being easy to find, and looking worth calling once you are found, is what changes that.
