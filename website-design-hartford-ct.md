# Hartford website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Hartford and across the Hartford metro. If you are looking for website designers in Hartford, Connecticut, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Hartford metro

Businesses across the whole Hartford area work with us, in Hartford itself and in every town around it. None of it depends on how close you are to the city.

We serve businesses in the Capitol Planning Region, the most populated planning region in the Hartford metro, and in the Lower Connecticut River Valley Planning Region, the only other planning region in the metro. Plenty of customers live in one and work or shop in the other, so a good site speaks to both.

## The insurance capital and the towns around it

Hartford has long been called the Insurance Capital of the World, and finance and insurance still shape its economy. Professional firms here need sites that look as careful as their work: clear credentials, clear people pages, clear next steps.

Federal statistics now use planning regions instead of Connecticut's old counties, but customers search for towns such as West Hartford, Glastonbury, Manchester and Middletown. Name those.

Hartford also has a large Puerto Rican community, and many local businesses serve customers in Spanish. If that includes you, real Spanish pages will serve them better than a translation button.

## Why a Hartford business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.
