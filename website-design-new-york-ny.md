## Use the borough names people search

Three of the five boroughs go by different names as counties: Brooklyn is Kings County, Manhattan is New York County and Staten Island is Richmond County. Customers search for the borough and the neighborhood, never the county, so your site should use those names.

The metro takes in 22 counties across New York and New Jersey, from Westchester and Long Island to Newark and Jersey City. Naming the places you genuinely serve is what makes customers in them pick up the phone.

A Manhattan storefront competes for foot traffic and map results, while many Brooklyn and Queens businesses draw from a handful of nearby neighborhoods. Write for the blocks your customers actually walk.
