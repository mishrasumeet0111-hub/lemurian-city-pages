# Website design for Sioux Falls businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Sioux Falls and across the Sioux Falls metro. If you are looking for website designers in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Sioux Falls metro

We work with businesses everywhere in the Sioux Falls metro, from the center of Sioux Falls to the towns at the far edge of it. Where you are based does not change what we can build for you.

Customers of a business in Minnehaha County, the most populated county in the Sioux Falls metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: Lincoln County, Turner County and McCook County in South Dakota; and Rock County in Minnesota. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the counties where most of the Sioux Falls metro lives. Between them, they make up the whole Sioux Falls metro.

## A regional center for health care and banking

Sioux Falls is South Dakota's largest city and a regional hub for health care and banking, with Sanford Health and Avera based here and several card issuers drawn by the state's lending laws. Firms serving those sectors need sites that look as solid as their clients.

The metro also reaches into Rock County, Minnesota. Harrisburg, Tea and Brandon have grown quickly, and their residents search for them by name.

Iowa and Minnesota are both a short drive away, and many customers come from across those lines. A clear service area saves them from wondering whether you reach them.
