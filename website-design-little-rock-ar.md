# Website design for Little Rock businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Little Rock and across the Little Rock metro. If you are looking for website designers in Little Rock, Arkansas, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Little Rock metro

The whole Little Rock region is served, city and suburbs alike. A business twenty miles out gets built exactly as carefully as one downtown.

Our Little Rock service area starts in Pulaski County, the most populated county in the Little Rock metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Faulkner County, Saline County, Lonoke County, Grant County and Perry County. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Little Rock metro.

## Both sides of the Arkansas River

Little Rock and North Little Rock sit on opposite banks of the Arkansas River, and Conway, Benton and Cabot each draw customers of their own. If you serve more than one, name them, because a search for one city does not always surface a business that only mentions another.

As the state capital, Little Rock has many businesses that work with state agencies, hospitals and nonprofits. Those visitors want credentials, contacts and documents quickly, so build the site around what they come for.
