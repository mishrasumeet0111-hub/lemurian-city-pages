# A web design company for Memphis and nearby counties

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Memphis and across the Memphis metro. If you are looking for website designers in Memphis, Tennessee, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Memphis metro

Businesses across the whole Memphis area work with us, in Memphis itself and in every town around it. None of it depends on how close you are to the city.

Memphis sits in Shelby County, the most populated county in the Memphis metro. Your customers may not, which is why we also work with businesses across the surrounding counties with the most people, Tipton County and Fayette County in Tennessee; DeSoto County, Marshall County, Tate County, Tunica County and Benton County in Mississippi; and Crittenden County in Arkansas, on exactly the same terms. Between them, they make up the whole Memphis metro.

## Three states meet here

The Memphis metro reaches into Mississippi and Arkansas, and Southaven and Olive Branch, in DeSoto County, are among its largest suburbs. If you serve more than one state, say so.

Memphis is a major logistics center, home to FedEx's global hub, and music and barbecue bring visitors year-round. Visitor-facing sites need directions, hours and booking up front; logistics suppliers need clear capability pages.

St. Jude Children's Research Hospital and other medical centers make health care a major employer alongside FedEx. Businesses serving hospital staff and visiting families need clear directions, hours and parking information.

## What a good site does for a Memphis business

Customers in Memphis search before they buy, and a business they cannot find online is a business they never call. A clear site makes you visible to them and brings the work in.
