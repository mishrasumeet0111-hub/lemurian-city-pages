# Jacksonville website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Jacksonville and across the Jacksonville metro. If you are looking for website designers in Jacksonville, Florida, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Jacksonville metro

From Jacksonville itself out to the smallest towns in the metro, every business gets the same service. Being outside the city limits costs you nothing here.

Customers of a business in Duval County, the most populated county in the Jacksonville metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: St. Johns County, Clay County, Nassau County and Baker County. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the counties where most of the Jacksonville metro lives. Between them, they make up the whole Jacksonville metro. That takes in places like Jacksonville Beach, Orange Park, St. Augustine, Fernandina Beach and Macclenny.

## One city, five counties

Duval County and the City of Jacksonville share one government, so a customer in Mandarin, Arlington or Riverside may think of themselves as living in Jacksonville. A customer in Orange Park, St. Augustine or Fernandina Beach usually does not. If you serve those places, your site should name them on the pages where they matter, instead of hoping "Jacksonville" covers them.

Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30. A site you can update yourself, with a notice bar for storm closures and changed hours, saves a phone call to your web designer when you need it least.

## The cost of being hard to find in Jacksonville

Most of the customers you are missing are the ones who went looking for a business like yours and found somebody else instead. A proper website closes that gap.
