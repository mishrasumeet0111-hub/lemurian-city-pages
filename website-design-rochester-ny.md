# Rochester website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Rochester and across the Rochester metro. If you are looking for website designers in Rochester, New York, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Rochester metro

We work with businesses everywhere in the Rochester metro, from the center of Rochester to the towns at the far edge of it. Where you are based does not change what we can build for you.

Rochester sits in Monroe County, the most populated county in the Rochester metro. Your customers may not, which is why we also work with businesses across the surrounding counties with the most people, Ontario County, Wayne County, Livingston County and Orleans County, on exactly the same terms. Between them, they make up the whole Rochester metro.

## An imaging and optics town

Rochester grew up around Kodak, Xerox and Bausch + Lomb, and optics, imaging and photonics are still a big part of the local economy. Technical firms here need sites that explain their capabilities plainly to engineers and buyers.

Customers come from Greece and Irondequoit as well as Canandaigua and Geneseo. Name the towns you serve.

The University of Rochester and RIT bring students and researchers, and many businesses serve them through the school year. Offers and hours you can change each semester keep the site accurate.

## What a good site does for a Rochester business

Customers in Rochester search before they buy, and a business they cannot find online is a business they never call. A clear site makes you visible to them and brings the work in.
