# Portland, OR website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Portland and across the Portland metro. If you are looking for website designers in Portland, Oregon, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Portland metro

Businesses across the whole Portland area work with us, in Portland, OR itself and in every town around it. None of it depends on how close you are to the city.

Our Portland, OR service area starts in Multnomah County, the most populated county in the Portland metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Washington County, Clackamas County, Yamhill County and Columbia County in Oregon; and Clark County and Skamania County in Washington. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Portland metro.

## Oregon and Washington, one metro

The Portland metro crosses the Columbia River into Washington, through Vancouver in Clark County. Oregon has no sales tax and Washington has no state income tax, so plenty of people live on one side and shop on the other. If you serve both, say so.

Locals talk in quadrants and neighborhoods: Northeast, Southeast, the Pearl, Alberta. Suburbs like Beaverton, Hillsboro, Gresham and Lake Oswego are markets of their own.

Portland customers tend to favor local, independent businesses and like to know who they are buying from. An about page with real people, real photos and your story does more here than in most cities.
