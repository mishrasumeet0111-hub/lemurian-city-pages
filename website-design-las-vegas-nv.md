## Locals and the Strip are different customers

The metro runs from Henderson and North Las Vegas to Summerlin and Boulder City. Many businesses serve either locals or visitors, and a site that tries to serve both at once usually serves neither well. Decide who the main audience is and build for them.

Visitors book from their phones, often at the last minute. If you take reservations, the booking button belongs in the first screen, and the site should load quickly on hotel Wi-Fi.

## The cost of being hard to find in Las Vegas
