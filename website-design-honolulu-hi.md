# Website design for Honolulu businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Honolulu and across the Honolulu metro. If you are looking for website designers in Honolulu, Hawaii, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Honolulu metro

All of the Honolulu metro is covered, so a shop on the outskirts gets the same attention as one in the middle of Honolulu.

We serve businesses across the City and County of Honolulu, which on its own makes up the whole Honolulu metro, from the center of Honolulu out to the shoreline.

## Locals and visitors on one site

The City and County of Honolulu covers all of Oahu, from Kapolei to Kailua to the North Shore. Many businesses serve both residents and visitors, who need different things: residents want hours and specials, visitors want directions, parking and booking.

Hawaii does not observe daylight saving time, so it is two or three hours behind the West Coast depending on the season. Booking tools need to run on Hawaii time, or visitors booking from the mainland will arrive at the wrong hour.

## Why a Honolulu business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.
