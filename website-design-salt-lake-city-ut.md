# Website design for Salt Lake City businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Salt Lake City and across the Salt Lake City metro. If you are looking for website designers in Salt Lake City, Utah, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Salt Lake City metro

Businesses across the whole Salt Lake City area work with us, in Salt Lake City itself and in every town around it. None of it depends on how close you are to the city.

We serve businesses in Salt Lake County, the most populated county in the Salt Lake City metro, and in Tooele County, the only other county in the metro. Plenty of customers live in one and work or shop in the other, so a good site speaks to both.

## The county, not just the city

Salt Lake City is small next to Salt Lake County, which includes West Valley City, Sandy, West Jordan and South Jordan. Customers search for their own city, so name the ones you serve.

Utah's tech corridor, often called Silicon Slopes, runs south toward Lehi and Provo, and many local businesses sell to software firms and startups. Those buyers expect a fast, modern site.

Ski and outdoor tourism bring visitors in winter and summer, heading for the Cottonwood canyons and Park City. If you serve them, seasonal hours and booking should be easy to change.

## Why a Salt Lake City business needs a site that works

A business without a website loses customers to the one down the street that has a good one, because that is where people look first. A site built properly puts you in front of those customers and turns them into paying work.
