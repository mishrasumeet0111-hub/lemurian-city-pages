# Website design for Kansas City businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Kansas City and across the Kansas City metro. If you are looking for website designers in Kansas City, Missouri, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Kansas City metro

Anywhere in the Kansas City area is fair game, whether you trade in Kansas City proper or out in the surrounding towns. The distance between us is never the point.

Customers of a business in Jackson County, the most populated county in the Kansas City metro, often live elsewhere in the metro: Clay County, Cass County, Platte County, Lafayette County and Ray County in Missouri; and Johnson County, Wyandotte County, Leavenworth County and Miami County in Kansas. We work with businesses in all of them, because those are the counties where most of the Kansas City metro lives. The Kansas City metro has 14 counties in all; these are the ten with the most residents in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates.

## Two states, one city name

There is a Kansas City, Missouri and a Kansas City, Kansas, and the metro takes in Johnson and Wyandotte County on the Kansas side. If you serve both states, say which, and list any licenses that apply on each side.

Johnson County, Kansas, home to Overland Park and Olathe, is a big market of its own. Customers there search for their own city, not Kansas City.

Kansas City is known for barbecue and jazz, and its restaurants and venues draw visitors from across the region. Menus, hours and event dates you can change yourself keep a busy site accurate.
