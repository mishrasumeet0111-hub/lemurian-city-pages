# Austin website designers, working remotely

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Austin and across the Austin metro. If you are looking for website designers in Austin, Texas, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Austin metro

We work with businesses everywhere in the Austin metro, from the center of Austin to the towns at the far edge of it. Where you are based does not change what we can build for you.

Our Austin service area starts in Travis County, the most populated county in the Austin metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Williamson County, Hays County, Bastrop County and Caldwell County. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Austin metro. That takes in places like Round Rock, San Marcos, Georgetown, Cedar Park, Kyle and Bastrop.

## For founders who need to look ready

Austin has a large startup and small-tech scene, and founders there often need a site that looks credible to investors and early customers before the product is finished. That calls for a clear story, a strong first screen and a site the team can change weekly without a developer. Framer suits that well.

The metro reaches north into Williamson County, through Round Rock and Georgetown, and south into Hays County, through Kyle and San Marcos. If you serve the suburbs, say so. Many people there search for their own city, not Austin.
