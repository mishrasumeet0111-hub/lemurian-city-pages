# A web design company for Miami and nearby counties

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Miami and across the Miami metro. If you are looking for website designers in Miami, Florida, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Miami metro

The whole Miami region is served, city and suburbs alike. A business twenty miles out gets built exactly as carefully as one downtown.

We serve businesses in Miami-Dade County, the most populated county in the Miami metro, and in the most populated counties around it: Broward County and Palm Beach County. Customers rarely stop at a county line, so neither do we. Between them, they make up the whole Miami metro. That takes in places like Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach, Hialeah, Coral Gables and Boca Raton.

## Sites in English and Spanish

Many Miami businesses serve customers in both English and Spanish. If that is you, plan the second language from the start: real translated pages with their own addresses and language tags, not a machine-translation widget. A widget is quicker, but search engines cannot index it and customers can tell.

The metro runs up the coast through three counties. A business in Boca Raton and one in Coral Gables are both "Miami" to a map but not to their customers, so name the area you serve in plain words.

## Why a Miami business needs a site that works

Plenty of good Miami businesses stay small only because nobody can find them. Being easy to find, and looking worth calling once you are found, is what changes that.
