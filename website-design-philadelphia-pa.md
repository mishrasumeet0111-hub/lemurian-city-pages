## Neighborhoods first

Philadelphians identify with their neighborhoods, like Fishtown, South Philly, Manayunk or Germantown, and they search that way. If you have a storefront, your neighborhood belongs in your page title and first screen. If you travel to customers, say how far you go, because a Roxborough customer and a Society Hill customer measure distance very differently.

The four suburban Pennsylvania counties, Montgomery, Bucks, Delaware and Chester, together hold more people than the city. The metro also takes in South Jersey, northern Delaware and Cecil County, Maryland. Name the suburbs and states you actually cover.

Universities and hospitals, including Penn and Jefferson, anchor the economy, and Center City is full of law and professional firms. Those buyers compare several firms before calling, so make your specialty and your people easy to see.
