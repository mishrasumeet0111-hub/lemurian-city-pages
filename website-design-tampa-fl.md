# Website design for Tampa businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Tampa and across the Tampa metro. If you are looking for website designers in Tampa, Florida, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Tampa metro

We work with businesses everywhere in the Tampa metro, from the center of Tampa to the towns at the far edge of it. Where you are based does not change what we can build for you.

Tampa sits in Hillsborough County, the most populated county in the Tampa metro. Your customers may not, which is why we also work with businesses across the surrounding counties with the most people, Pinellas County, Pasco County and Hernando County, on exactly the same terms. Between them, they make up the whole Tampa metro. That takes in places like St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Wesley Chapel and Spring Hill.

## Both sides of the bay

The Tampa metro splits across the bay: Tampa and Brandon in Hillsborough County, St. Petersburg and Clearwater in Pinellas. Customers on one side often search with their own city name. If you serve both, your site should make that obvious, with each side named where it matters.

Gulf Coast storms can close a business for days. Build the site so you can post closures, reopen dates and changed hours yourself, without waiting on anyone.

## What a good site does for a Tampa business

People trust what they can see. A business with no site, or a tired one, gets passed over by customers who would happily have bought from it.

## A question from Tampa businesses

### Should I have separate pages for Tampa and St. Petersburg?

Only if you have something different to say about each, such as different locations, staff or services. Two pages that differ only by city name help no one, and Google treats them as doorway pages.
