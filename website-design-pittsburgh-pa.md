# Website design for Pittsburgh businesses

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Pittsburgh and across the Pittsburgh metro. If you are looking for website designers in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Pittsburgh metro

Anywhere in the Pittsburgh area is fair game, whether you trade in Pittsburgh proper or out in the surrounding towns. The distance between us is never the point.

We serve businesses in Allegheny County, the most populated county in the Pittsburgh metro, and in the most populated counties around it: Westmoreland County, Washington County, Butler County, Beaver County, Fayette County, Lawrence County and Armstrong County. Customers rarely stop at a county line, so neither do we. Between them, they make up the whole Pittsburgh metro.

## Hills, rivers and neighborhoods

Pittsburgh's hills and rivers split it into dozens of distinct neighborhoods, and locals search by them: Lawrenceville, Shadyside, Squirrel Hill, the South Side. A storefront should lead with its neighborhood.

The region's economy has shifted toward health care, universities and technology, with UPMC, Carnegie Mellon and Pitt as anchors. Businesses that serve those institutions need sites that explain their work clearly and look as current as their clients.

Allegheny County alone has about 130 municipalities, and people identify with theirs, from Mt. Lebanon to Monroeville. Name the boroughs and townships you serve, and Cranberry Township in Butler County if it is in your area.

## Why a Pittsburgh business needs a site that works

Plenty of good Pittsburgh businesses stay small only because nobody can find them. Being easy to find, and looking worth calling once you are found, is what changes that.
