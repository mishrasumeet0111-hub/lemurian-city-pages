# A web design company for Denver and nearby counties

Lemurian Studios designs and builds websites, brand identities and 3D animation for businesses in Denver and across the Denver metro. If you are looking for website designers in Denver, Colorado, this page explains how we work with businesses there.

## Web design across the Denver metro

We work with businesses everywhere in the Denver metro, from the center of Denver to the towns at the far edge of it. Where you are based does not change what we can build for you.

Our Denver service area starts in Denver County, the most populated county in the Denver metro, and runs through the metro's other most populated counties: Arapahoe County, Jefferson County, Adams County, Douglas County, Broomfield County, Elbert County, Park County, Clear Creek County and Gilpin County. Wherever your business is in that ring, the process is the same. Between them, they make up the whole Denver metro.

## From the city to the Front Range suburbs

Denver is its own county, but most of the metro lives in the counties around it. Customers in Aurora, Lakewood, Littleton or Castle Rock search for their own city, so name the ones you serve.

Outdoor and active-lifestyle businesses are a big part of the region, and their sites live or die on photography. Plan for real photos of your place, your people and your customers, not stock images of mountains.

## Being found by Denver customers

The work is already out there in Denver; it goes to whoever shows up when people search. A site built to be found is how you get your share of it.
